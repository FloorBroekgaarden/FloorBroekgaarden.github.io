## San Diego and the Astronomy Department at UCSD

### UC San Diego & the Astronomy Department

The UCSD Astronomy department represents a dynamic research community. As a newly independent department (separated from Physics), it has recently recruited over five new faculty members, strengthening its already collaborative environment. The department encompasses experts in theory, observations, data science, and instrumentation.

Key resources include:
- [UCSD Astronomy website](https://astro.ucsd.edu/)
- [Research areas](https://astro.ucsd.edu/research/areas/index.html)
- [Faculty and staff directory](https://astro.ucsd.edu/people/index.html)

The institution benefits from proximity to the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) and the Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute, plus collaborative partnerships with nearby institutions like San Diego State University.

### San Diego

The city offers year-round sunshine, beaches, outdoor recreation including surfing and volleyball, and wildlife viewing. It provides vibrant urban amenities with cultural venues, dining, and shopping alongside natural attractions including the San Diego Zoo and Balboa Park.

## Join UCSD & my group for your PhD!

Dr. Broekgaarden welcomes PhD applications. Prospective students should apply through: [https://astro.ucsd.edu/grad/admissions/index.html](https://astro.ucsd.edu/grad/admissions/index.html)

Fellowship opportunities include NSF, NASA FINESST, Hertz, and Department of Energy programs (some with October 15, 2024 deadlines).

Research interests span "stellar evolution, neutron stars & black holes, basically anything gravitational waves, astro-statistics, AI, simulations, data science, white dwarfs, pulsars, kilonovae, GRBs & SNe, and many more topics."

## Join UCSD & my group for your Postdoc!

Postdoc positions are available. Eligible candidates may pursue external fellowships including NSF Postdoc Fellowship, NHFP Hubble Fellowship, UCSD Schmidt AI in Science Fellowship, UC President's Postdoctoral Fellowship Program, and L'Oréal USA for Women in Science Fellowship.
