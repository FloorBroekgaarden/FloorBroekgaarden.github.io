<p><strong>SMASH</strong> — <em>UC San Diego Meetings between Astro/physics, SDSC &amp; HDSI</em> — is a UCSD-wide initiative that brings together researchers from the Department of Astronomy &amp; Astrophysics, the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC), and the Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute (HDSI) to foster collaborations at the intersection of astrophysics, AI, and data science.</p>

<p>The initiative was launched in October 2024 and has since grown into a vibrant community spanning physicists, astronomers, data scientists, and computer scientists at UC San Diego. SMASH activities include research meetings, interdisciplinary workshops, and hands-on hackathons — including the annual UCSD SMASH &amp; NSF HDR ML Hackathon, which challenges participants to apply machine learning to real scientific problems.</p>

<p>SMASH is supported by the UCSD VCRI Convene and Influence Award and NSF HDR funding. It is co-organized by Prof. Floor Broekgaarden (Astronomy &amp; Astrophysics), Prof. Javier Duarte (Physics), Prof. Liang Yang (HDSI/CSE), and colleagues across UCSD.</p>
